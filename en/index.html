The game

What is the game about?

You appear on a round planet with green plains, mountains you can climb and an underground full of dirt, rock and veins of gold. The world is home to wolves that hunt in packs, bears that defend their territory and villages with villagers ready to fight… if you pay them well.

There are no teams: it's everyone against everyone. Your goal is to gather the most powerful army, whether by taming wild animals or striking deals with villagers, and hold on to it against the other players. You can dig, build, explore, fight and negotiate: you decide how to reach the top.

The game's interface is in Spanish; in this guide each button's on-screen name appears in parentheses so you can recognize it.

First steps

How to start playing

  1. Type your name and press Entrar (Enter). No sign-up needed.
  2. Look around. You start on a quiet plain, with mountains in sight.
  3. Try your tools: hit the ground, collect material and build something.
  4. Find your first ally: an animal to tame or a village to strike a deal with.

The game remembers your device: when you come back from the same browser, you'll be the same player and get your army back.

Controls

How to play on PC and mobile

On PC

  • W A S D: walk. Space: jump. Mouse: look around.
  • Click: use the tool in your hand (hold to repeat).
  • 1 to 7 or the mouse wheel: switch tools.
  • Q: your army follows you or stays put. E: your army attacks what you're aiming at.
  • C: open the chat. Esc: release the mouse.

On mobile

  • Put your finger on the left side of the screen and a joystick will appear for walking.
  • Drag anywhere else on the screen to look around.
  • Buttons Use (Usar), Jump (Saltar), Follow me/Stay (Seguidme/Quedaos) and Attack (Atacar).
  • The tools are at the bottom; if they don't fit, swipe them sideways (the arrows let you know there are more).
  • You can play in portrait or landscape, whichever feels more comfortable.

Tools

Your tools

  • Hand (Mano): hit whoever is in front of you or dig the ground.
  • Stone (Piedra): the same, but with more strength.
  • Throw (Lanzar): throw stones at a distance; they fall to the ground and you can pick them up again.
  • Dirt (Tierra) and Rock (Roca): place blocks to raise walls, stairs or shelters.
  • Tame (Domar): turn a weakened animal into part of your army.
  • Deal (Pactar): pay a villager to fight by your side.

You start with some dirt and stones. Gold is found by digging deep, among the rock, and it's the currency villagers value most.

Your character

Health, energy and strength

At the top center you'll see a bar: the left half is your health and the right half your energy. Every hit, every stone thrown and every animal tamed costs energy, which recovers on its own in a few seconds. Health also comes back little by little. If you fall, you respawn right away, so don't be afraid to experiment.

Each hit deals random damage based on your strength (between 1 and 5 at the start), and holding a stone in your hand increases it.

The planet

Digging and building

The planet can be shaped block by block. Dirt gives way in a few hits, rock holds out much longer and gold is the hardest of all. Every block you break goes into your inventory: dirt gives you dirt, and rock gives you stones.

With that material you can build: steps to climb a mountain, a wall to hold off the wolves or a tower from which to watch over your army. If you dig a hole under your feet, jump to get out.

Your army

How to build your army

Taming animals

Wolves are fast and travel in packs; bears are slow but very powerful. To tame them, you first have to weaken them: when they have little health left (less than a third), choose Domar (Tame) and get close. From then on they'll follow and protect you.

Striking deals with villagers

Villages are home to villagers with different trades. If you pay their price in materials, they join you:

  • Warrior: tough and strong in close combat. Costs 3 gold and 5 stones.
  • Archer: attacks from afar by throwing stones. Costs 2 gold and 10 stones.
  • Miner: more modest, but cheap. Costs 10 dirt.

Giving orders

Orders are simple: have your army follow you, stay guarding a spot or attack whatever you're aiming at. A good leader knows when to advance and when to defend.

How to win

Becoming king of the world

Every unit in your army adds power: a bear is worth more than a wolf, and a warrior more than a miner. Your total power is the sum of your whole army, and the top players board shows who's ahead. Whoever holds first place is the king of the world and wears a crown over their head for everyone to see.

But the crown isn't guaranteed. When you log off, your army stays in the world guarding its spot, and other players can attack it, tame your animals or win over your villagers with a better offer. Keeping power is as important as getting it.

On the top board, the circle next to each name works like a clock: full if the player is online, and it empties with the hours they've been away.

Community

The chat

At the top right (or below the top board on mobile) you'll see world notices: who joins, who leaves, who defeated whom, who tamed or struck a deal. With the Chat button you can open the history and review everything that has happened. You'll also see there, just for you, your strength and the state of your army.

Tips

Tips to get off to a good start

  • Before taking on a bear, start with a wolf: it's easier to weaken.
  • Hold a stone in your hand to fight and dig faster.
  • Dig deep to find gold: it's the key to striking deals with warriors and archers.
  • Watch your energy: if it runs out, wait a few seconds before hitting again.
  • Use the mountains and what you build to defend yourself from the packs.
  • Once you have a good army, order it to stay somewhere safe before logging off.

FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Is DJC G1 free?

Yes. You play it for free in your browser, with nothing to download and no account to create.

Can I play on my phone?

Yes. On mobile you play with a joystick and touch buttons, in portrait or landscape.

What happens to my army if I log off?

It stays in the world guarding its spot. When you come back from the same device you get it back, although other players may try to take it from you while you're away.

Can I play with my friends?

Yes: all players share the same planet. Join at the same time and you'll meet in the same world… just remember that, in the end, there can only be one king.

The game is still growing

DJC G1 is still in development and every update brings something new. Some updates may restart the world, so make the most of every match.
